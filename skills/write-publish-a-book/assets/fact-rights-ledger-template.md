## Status rules

- **Confirmed:** supported by the author or a reliable source and safe to use within its stated boundary.
- **Unverified:** retain the editorial structure but do not state as fact.
- **Opinion:** attribute clearly and do not present as universal evidence.
- **Teaching construction:** label explicitly; never convert it into author biography.
