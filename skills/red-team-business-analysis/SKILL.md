# Red-team a business analysis

Attack decision risk, not writing style.

## Workflow

1. Identify decision, audience, strongest claim and highest-cost action.
2. Challenge framing, source/definition, sample, window, causality, outliers, baseline, extrapolation, model, AI, claim strength, stakeholders, cost/side effects, implementation resistance, and privacy.
3. Rank by likelihood × decision impact.
4. For each, write why, prevention, evidence, 30-second answer, and downgraded answer.
5. Flag questions needing new authority or evidence.

## Output and boundaries

Return top-three main-deck risks, full challenge table, evidence plan, and claims to weaken/remove. Never help talk around missing evidence, fabricate sources, or conceal privacy/compliance. Downgrade, defer or withdraw when needed.

Use [test cases](references/test-cases.md) when validating changes.
