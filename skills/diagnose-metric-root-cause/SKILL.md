# Diagnose a metric change

Separate description, contribution, mechanism, correlation, and causality.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the metric card and data-quality verdict.
2. Build an additive bridge and reconcile parts to total.
3. Locate concentration by segment, channel, cohort, product or process step.
4. Propose mechanisms and observable predictions.
5. Write at least two alternatives and actively test them.
6. Grade each claim strong, medium, or weak.
7. Recommend the smallest experiment, quasi-experiment, source, or signal needed next.

## Output and boundaries

Return the bridge, issue tree, claim-evidence table, rejected explanations, uncertainty, and verification plan. Do not call a factor root cause from correlation, 5 Whys, or before/after comparison alone. If quality is not fit, return to quality assessment.

Use [test cases](references/test-cases.md) when validating changes.
